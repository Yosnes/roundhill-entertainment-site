SMS consent notice

This page documents every way customers give Round Hill Entertainment permission to send text messages.

Program: Round Hill Entertainment customer communications · Number: +1 (818) 927-6334 · Effective: August 26, 2026

Consumer-initiated text messages

Customers may initiate a conversation by texting +1 (818) 927-6334, a number published on this website. The customer’s inbound message gives Round Hill Entertainment permission to respond to that inquiry and its related conversation. It does not enroll the customer in unrelated promotional or recurring messages.

To expressly subscribe to recurring informational messages concerning an active inquiry or project, a customer texts START to +1 (818) 927-6334.

Verbal consent

During a telephone conversation, an authorized Round Hill Entertainment representative reads:

“Would you like to receive text messages from Round Hill Entertainment concerning your inquiry, estimates, scheduling, project updates, and other requested service communications? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe or HELP for help.”

The customer must clearly answer yes before being enrolled. Round Hill Entertainment retains the customer’s telephone number, date, time, and method of consent.

Confirmation

After an express verbal or START opt-in, the customer receives:

“Round Hill Entertainment: You’re subscribed to customer communications about your inquiry or project. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe.”

Message details

  • Messages may include replies to inquiries, estimate coordination, scheduling, project updates, and other requested service information.
  • Message frequency varies based on the customer’s conversation or project.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.
  • Round Hill Entertainment does not use purchased lists, affiliate leads, or transferred consent.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance or email info@roundhillentertainment.com.

Mobile information, including telephone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent, will not be sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.
