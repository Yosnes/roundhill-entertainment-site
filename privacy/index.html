Privacy policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Round Hill Entertainment LLC d/b/a Round Hill Consulting (“Round Hill Consulting,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects and uses information when you visit this website or communicate with us.

Effective: August 26, 2026 · Contact: info@roundhillentertainment.com

Notwithstanding anything else in this policy, mobile information—including telephone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent—will not be sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent is provided directly to Round Hill Entertainment and applies only to messages sent by Round Hill Entertainment.

Information you provide

We may collect information you choose to provide when you contact us, request information, discuss a potential engagement, or communicate with us by email, telephone, or text message. This may include your name, organization, email address, telephone number, message content, project details, and communication preferences.

SMS and mobile information

If you communicate with Round Hill Consulting by SMS, we may collect your mobile telephone number, message content, consent records, opt-in method, opt-in date and time, and messaging preferences.

We use this information only to respond to inquiries and provide estimates, scheduling, project updates, and other service-related communications you request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

We do not buy or sell SMS consent, use purchased messaging lists, or transfer consent between businesses.

Website information

Our hosting and security providers may process basic technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, and timestamps. We do not use this website to collect sensitive personal information.

How we use information

  • Respond to questions and requests.
  • Discuss, plan, and provide consulting services.
  • Send requested service-related communications.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Maintain business records and improve our services.

Service providers

We may use service providers for website hosting, communications, security, and other business operations. They may process information only as necessary to provide services to us and subject to confidentiality obligations.

Mobile telephone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent are never provided to service providers for their own marketing or promotional use.

Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal and recordkeeping requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete information you have provided, subject to applicable legal requirements. To stop SMS messages, reply STOP. For other privacy requests, email info@roundhillentertainment.com.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our practices or legal obligations change. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
